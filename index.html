How to read it. The horizontal axis is RS-Ratio — how a sector is performing relative to the benchmark, normalised so 100 is neutral. The vertical axis is RS-Momentum — the rate of change of that relative strength. Sectors rotate clockwise: Improving → Leading → Weakening → Lagging → Improving. Tails show the last N periods; the dot is the most recent one.

Prices from Yahoo Finance, daily closes. Educational tool — not investment advice.
